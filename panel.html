Capital & compounding

Fixed size: every trade is sized on the start amount, profits pile up untouched (good while you withdraw regularly). Compound profits: the bot sizes on start amount + closed P&L since the start − withdrawals + deposits, so trades grow as the account grows — this is how every backtest is calculated. When you take money out, press Record withdrawal so the bot sizes on what is really left (it also stops a withdrawal from looking like a loss to the daily halt / drawdown limits). How ZackBot works

    
    
      
  1. After each candle closes, ZackBot downloads fresh candles from Binance and evaluates every active strategy on every coin that is switched on.
    2. 
      
  2. When a strategy fires, it sizes the trade so that hitting the stop loses exactly the chosen risk % of that slot's capital.
    3. 
      
  3. It opens a market order and immediately places a hard stop on Binance, which works even if your PC is off.
    4. 
      
  4. Every few seconds it manages open trades: partial take-profits, breakeven, trailing stops, pyramiding adds and DCA safety orders.
    5. 
      
  5. Exits happen on the strategy's exit signal, on the stop or take-profit, or when you press Close/Flatten.
    6. 
      
  6. Daily loss halt and drawdown flatten protect the account; everything is logged in Trades and Logs.

    

      
R (R-multiple)
Profit divided by the money risked. Risked $10 and made $25 = +2.5R. Losing exactly the planned amount = −1R.

      
ATR
Average True Range — the coin's typical candle size. Stops measured in ATR adapt to each coin's volatility.

      
Risk per trade
% of the slot's capital lost if the stop is hit. Not the position size: a 3% risk trade can use 5-10× leverage.

      
Max drawdown
The biggest fall from a high point to a low point. The number that decides whether people stop copying you.

      
Hedge mode
Lets a long and a short on the same coin exist together (needed for short strategies).

      
Pyramiding / DCA
Pyramiding adds to winning trades; DCA (safety orders) adds to losing ones up to a hard basket stop.

      
Profit factor
Gross wins ÷ gross losses. Above 1.5 is good for trend systems.
