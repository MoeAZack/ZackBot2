Capital & compounding

Fixed size: every trade is sized on the start amount, profits pile up untouched (good while you withdraw regularly). Compound profits: the bot sizes on start amount + closed P&L since the start − withdrawals + deposits, so trades grow as the account grows — this is how every backtest is calculated. When you take money out, press Record withdrawal so the bot sizes on what is really left (it also stops a withdrawal from looking like a loss to the daily halt / drawdown limits). Active strategy slots

A grid bot cuts a price band into levels: it buys each level on the way down and sells it one level higher (neutral grids also short above the start price). It earns small amounts while price moves sideways and loses when price trends out of the band — then the hard stop beyond the band closes everything. Each slot gets its share of bot capital, split over up to "max coins" grids. The preview below shows the worst case before you save.

The bull-only market filter
Over 4.8 years it removed most of the 2022 loss and cut max drawdown by about a third at almost the same return. Over the last 2 years alone it cost return, because it sat out the 2026 recovery. It is a bear-market insurance, not free profit.
Active's weak spot
The 1h Breakout slot drove Active to a −50% drawdown over 4 years (Monte Carlo: 18% chance of falling below half the start). The 1h DCA slot alone made more with −26%. The 6-month "Tight ladder" win did not hold up.
Mixing uncorrelated slots
Half trend (bull-only, 4h) + half 1h DCA: max drawdown −15%, Monte Carlo median −11%, 5% worst −17%, no path fell below half the start. The best risk-adjusted result so far.

Lead-trader study · 85 top Binance lead traders, 5,782 closed positions

Letting winners run · every profile, $500 start, 2 years (Active: 6 months of 1h)

Each profile tested with different ways of handling winning trades. "Standard" already lets winners run — the EMA strategies have no profit target and only exit when the trend reverses, which is where the 100R+ trades come from. Moving the stop up tightly (breakeven at 1R, then 1R behind every new high) makes the equity curve smoother but cuts those big trends short: crypto often pulls back 2-4R inside a strong trend. The exception is the 1h Active profile: there a tight ladder (breakeven at 1R, stop 1R behind each new high) did best in a 6-month test - few variants survive that much testing, so treat it as a candidate. Results are compounded (no withdrawals).

4h vs 1h · same strategies, core 8 coins, last 6 months

Faster candles mean more trades, but only some strategies survive the noise. DCA dip, Breakout + pyramiding and Hot-coin improve on 1h; the EMA-cross strategies, pullback and shorts get worse. Over 4 years of 1h data only DCA dip held up (Breakout 1h: −83% max drawdown) — see Long history above.

How ZackBot works

  1. After each candle closes, ZackBot downloads fresh candles from Binance and evaluates every active strategy on every coin that is switched on.
  2. When a strategy fires, it sizes the trade so that hitting the stop loses exactly the chosen risk % of that slot's capital ("Trades sized on" in the header).
  3. It opens a market order and immediately places a hard stop on Binance, which works even if your PC is off. The safety bar shows "Unprotected" in red if a stop is not confirmed.
  4. Every few seconds it manages open trades: partial take-profits, breakeven, trailing stops, pyramiding adds and DCA safety orders.
  5. Exits happen on the strategy's exit signal, on the stop or take-profit, or when you press Close/Flatten.
  6. The daily loss halt stops new entries until midnight Cairo; drawdown flatten closes everything at a hard floor. Everything is logged in Trades and Logs.
