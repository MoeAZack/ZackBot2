# MPE-01 independent data audit

Scope: data only. No reproducer imported, no trading outcomes calculated. `audit.py` reads the frozen contract and quality report, hashes both 15-minute copies and canonical 1-hour files, validates bar continuity and OHLC/volume, and independently aggregates exactly four UTC-aligned 15-minute bars per complete hour. Prices are compared exactly using Decimal; base and quote volume differ only if relative error exceeds 1e-6. Per-symbol hashes, discrepancy values, and counts are in `result.json`.

All nine 15-minute files match their frozen contract hashes and the backup byte-for-byte; all nine canonical hourly files match the manifest, whose SHA-256 matches the contract. Each has 68,764 aligned, sequential 15-minute bars and 17,191 complete comparable hours. No duplicates, non-increasing timestamps, interval gaps, invalid OHLC, nonpositive base/quote volume, incomplete hours, or missing matching hours were found. Reported OHLC and base-volume mismatch counts agree with data_quality.json for every symbol.

All nine have the same four discrepancy hours: 28 October 2024 23:00 and 29 October 2024 00:00 Cairo (28 October 20:00 and 21:00 UTC), and 14 January 2025 15:00 and 17:00 Cairo (13:00 and 15:00 UTC). Each of the 36 symbol-hours differs in base and quote volume; some also differ in high, low, or close; opens match throughout. Full exact values are recorded in result.json. These differences can alter volume-based fill eligibility and price-based fill/exit behavior for affected orders or positions; this audit did not quantify that effect or assess outcomes.

The two 15-minute copies have identical bytes and therefore do not provide independent source provenance. The claim that the discrepancies originate in Binance's own interval data was not independently verified here. The audit verifies the observed differences, not which interval is authoritative. The result cannot establish profitability, execution realism, or protection.
