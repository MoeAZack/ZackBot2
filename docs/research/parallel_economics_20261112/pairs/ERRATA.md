# Pair convergence pilot: status and errata
Status FAILED_PRELIMINARY_SCREEN / PARKED, not independent qualification or definitive rejection of every pair strategy. Both dependent thresholds lost before and after modeled costs; no activation authorized.
The pilot used trailing 720h aggregate quote volume >=$10m, not the requested D01 median >=$10m per completed day over 30 UTC days. Its 7/15/23bp fees plus slippage were charged as all-in bps on reference notional rather than independently pricing 2bp adverse fills and fees on actual fill prices. Basket stop is modeled, not exchange protection, and gaps can exceed risk; a final open position can be dropped at the sample boundary. Those issues apply to both the original and post-outcome filter sensitivity; the filter sensitivity does not fix liquidity or fill mechanics. Both variants are a single dependent family, not independent alphas. Input ends 2026-09-27 and the current filter proxy is retrospectively applied. No holdout used.
