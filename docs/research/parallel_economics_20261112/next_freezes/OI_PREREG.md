# BTC/ETH open-interest participation: frozen research protocol
Freeze before OI counts or outcomes. Two dependent hypotheses in one family, evaluated as separate one-position books. Canonical price input H5-WIDE-D01 manifest SHA256 99af9bfb4a9b29ede80f8f3ff54541912d74d03b3b93d984432832c934bdfa95. BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT only, priority BTC then ETH. Research-only, no PAPER or deployment. Price and OI restricted to before 2026-09-28 00:00 UTC. Official daily 5-minute Binance USD-M metrics zip and sibling SHA256 checksums; archive creation/publication timing is not independently established. Archive availability at timestamp plus one hour is an assumption, not proof of real-time availability.
OI uses sum_open_interest contracts, not sum_open_interest_value. At completed price bar close T, last metrics snapshot <=T-1h and the snapshot exactly one hour earlier, each no older than five minutes relative to their required timestamp. Reject missing or duplicated timestamps and require all intervening 5-minute snapshots exactly present; no forward-filling gaps. Change=current/prior-1. OI1: absolute close-open >= mean true range of latest 14 completed bars and OI change <=-2%; enter opposite candle direction. OI2: OI change >=+2%, close > prior 24 completed highs and EMA50>EMA200 => long; close < prior 24 lows and EMA50<EMA200 => short. EMA is causal recursive with adjust=False and minimum 200 completed bars. At signal require prior 720 contiguous hourly bars with cumulative quote volume >=$10m, and complete current price bar. At most one global position per hypothesis, no pyramiding, next-open entry.
At entry fixed stop 3*ATR14, target 6*ATR14 (2R gross); expiry after 24 hourly bars, fill next open after expiry. On entry bar and later bars stop and target are tested on high/low; if both reached use stop. Stop gaps fill worse open; target gaps fill target (conservative). Charge on actual entry and exit notional 7/15/23 bp per side, representing 5/13/21 bp fee plus 2 bp adverse slippage; funding stress 1bp per 8h charged hourly on marked notional. Size quantity downward using current testnet exchangeInfo snapshot, with minimum quantity/notional enforced; size so 3ATR loss plus 46bp stressed round-trip and 3bp 24h funding is <= $8, gross notional <=$3330. Dynamic equity does not compound. Dollar 8 denotes modeled risk, gap losses can exceed it. Flat-only terminal mark: close any open position at last available close, charge exit costs; no unclosed trades dropped. No account access.
Report all variants and cost scenarios, gross, fee/slippage and funding, R, PF, frequency, marked drawdown, BTC/ETH and early(<2025)/later split. Both periods consumed development, not validation. Model outputs cannot establish profitability, publication-timing causality, exchange protection, or independent significance.
