# Pre-existing findings for roadmap placement (Claude, 2026-10-07)

None of these was caused by a merged or pending ticket. Each was found while reviewing other work.

## F1: Rate-limit hazards in `binance_client._req` (safety). A fix is prepared as the TRATE PR.

Both problems were reproduced on master 3b86a03, using a fake client and a patched sleep.

**H1: sleeping while holding the engine lock.**
- A cancel that gets a 429 with Retry-After 30 makes 4 calls and sleeps 90 s inside `_req` while `manage` holds `engine.lock`.
- During that time, stop restoration, flatten and panel actions are blocked.

**H2: an IP ban is cut short and retried.**
- A 418 with Retry-After 7200 is cut to 60 s and retried 3 times while still banned, which can make the ban longer.
- An order sent afterwards still went out.

**The fix** (local branch `rate-limit-hazards`, 5f2be6d, on 874cfd8, which is the same as master):
- **418:** never retried. A ban window is recorded from the uncut Retry-After, limited to 0 s–3 days.
- **During a ban:** every request fails at once without being sent (`RateLimitBan`). An order refused this way is a definite failure, never ambiguous.
- **429:** at most 2 s of sleep in total per request.
- **Incident:** one coalesced `rate-ban` incident, with one recovery line.
- **Tests:** 12 new tests; 15/15 deliberate code breakages caught.

**Not fixed, left for T10:**
- A 503 with Retry-After 30 can still hold the lock for about 90 s, for cancels and critical reads only.
- There is no cooldown after a 429.

## F2: Backtest keeps a zero-quantity position when `tp1_frac=1.0` (behaviour change; needs its own ticket and golden re-record)

- **Where:** `backtest.py` around line 350. When tp1 closes the whole position, `close()` returns True and records the trade, but the return value is ignored. A zero-quantity position stays open.
- **Effects:**
  - It holds a `max_pos` slot (one case lasted 52 candles).
  - A pyramid add can revive it.
  - The trade is then closed again, so trade rows and the Kelly history are counted twice.
  - `ladder_frac_backtest` divides by zero.
- **The live engine is correct**, so this is also a gap between engine and backtest results.
- **Repro:** with `tp1_frac=1.0`, 9 trades include 3 duplicate rows. With 0.5 or 0.999 there are none.
- **Smallest fix:** `if close(...): del sl['pos'][s]; continue` before `p['tp1'] = True`.
- **Placement:** T09a PR 1 (needed before any full-partial-close experiment).

## F3: Core differential-test coverage worth adding (T06–T09 follow-up)

The adversarial review found no behaviour change in T06–T09. It compared bit-exact floats over 1,160 live scenarios, 450 dry scenarios, about 57k `manage()` passes, 290 backtest runs and 47 grid runs.

Suggested additions:
1. A bit-exact hex-float differential as a slow test. The golden test rounds to 1e-9.
2. Pin `tests/test_core_state.py` to verbatim original code, not to `core.levels` helpers.
3. Golden scenarios for:
   - pending and ambiguous orders;
   - the outage add-block;
   - the breaker's half-size and pause policies;
   - the dust rule;
   - manual lots;
   - a runner bank with `dca_frac` below 1;
   - the state-save count.
4. A backtest regression test for tp1 closing the full position (F2).
